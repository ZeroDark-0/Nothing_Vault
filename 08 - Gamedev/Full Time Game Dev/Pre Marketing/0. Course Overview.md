## <code style = "color:red">1.SOCIAL MEDIA PRESENCE</code>
Building up social media - YT, twitter, facebook, instagram, tiktok or steam page.

## <code style = "color:red">2.PROTOYPING</code>
Get the ideas of game, the mechanic, the design and other thing..
look at the mistake and show other people/friends,. getr recommendation from people..

## <code style = "color:red">3.GAME DESIGN BIBLE</code>
What is game about...
get all the element, the bury view of the game...

## <code style = "color:red">4.VERTICAL SLICE</code>
like trailer, mini version of the game, or a web page for that..

## <code style = "color:lime">5.FUNDING(OPTIONAL)</code>
securing fund with publisher or crowd funding...

## <code style = "color:red">6.EXTENDING PROTOTYPE</code>
extend the game, it's element like expanding the game..
scale and scale and scale.....

## <code style = "color:red">7.DEMO LANCH</code>
get a polished demo of the game, what the game is, what is all about...
(this is also increase the wishlist)

## <code style = "color:red">8.BETA TESTING</code>
looking for glitch, bugs
the pro and the cons..

## <code style = "color:red">9.BUILD HYPE</code>
stay active on social media, posting about the game, interacting and building up the hype..

## <code style = "color:red">10.STEAM LAUCH!</code>
and finally the launch of the game!!!.. \
